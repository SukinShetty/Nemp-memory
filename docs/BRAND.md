# Nemp identity and documentation

## Name and message

- **Product:** Nemp
- **Tagline:** Agentic memory that evolves with your work
- **Current edition:** Nemp Open v0.3.0
- **Future edition:** Nemp Pro, in development
- **Repository/plugin identifiers:** `Nemp-memory`, `nemp-memory` and `nemp` remain unchanged for compatibility

Describe what people can do: capture useful context, retrieve it, review it and refine it as work changes. Explain that today's loop is agent-guided. Do not turn the tagline into a claim of autonomous learning.

## Visual system

Use Nemp's purple/blue brain-and-chip mark. Keep host-agent brands in integration sections rather than in Nemp's hero identity. Use a small mark, live-text heading and short first-screen copy. Favor readable documentation over a large promotional banner.

The README reuses the original Nemp artwork unchanged. The lifecycle diagram is a code-native SVG. Original banners and demo captures are retained as historical assets, but the old co-branded banners are not used in the README.

## Claims checklist

Use “local memory” or “local storage,” not an unqualified “100% local” processing claim. Use a static package-version badge, not a latest-release badge that may show a different historical release. Separate current behavior, experimental instructions and planned features. Avoid “works on every platform,” “all operations are audited,” “never loses context,” and unmeasured token or performance claims.

Do not claim that entering a key unlocks Pro until verification and feature delivery exist and are tested.
